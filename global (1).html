    Region guide

    How each region works.

    Demand drivers, buying behaviour, channel structure, entry considerations and typical risks, region by region.

    Middle East

    Fast-moving distributor and project markets with strong Indian supplier familiarity.

    Demand drivers
    • Industrial, infrastructure and project activity
    • Strong familiarity with Indian suppliers
    • Fast-moving, distributor-led demand
    Buying behaviour
    • Decisions can move quickly
    • Relationships and price both matter
    • Distributors and project contractors often influence who is specified
    Channel structure
    • Distributor- and project-led
    • Coverage often grows step by step from a base market
    Entry considerations
    • Choose the distributor model deliberately, not by whoever enquires first
    • Set targets and governance from the start
    • Price for channel margins and freight
    Typical risks
    • Opportunistic, distributor-led growth that is hard to scale
    • Passive distributors who carry the brand without activating it
    • Margin erosion from competing on price alone
    Trade agreements, as of 15 Oct 2026
    • UAE: CEPA in force since 1 May 2022
    • Oman: CEPA in force since 1 June 2026
    • GCC: free trade agreement under negotiation

    Africa

    Growing infrastructure and industrial demand, with fragmented channels.

    Demand drivers
    • Growing infrastructure and industrial demand
    • An expanding industrial base across many countries
    Buying behaviour
    • Varies sharply from country to country
    • Availability, price and local credibility all weigh
    • After-sales support can decide the order
    Channel structure
    • Fragmented, with many small distributors and traders
    • Coverage is uneven, even within one country
    Entry considerations
    • Focus on a few countries rather than the whole continent
    • Map the channel country by country before appointing anyone
    • Plan logistics and support alongside the sale
    Typical risks
    • Fragmented channels that are hard to govern
    • Uneven payment and delivery conditions
    • Spreading effort too thin
    Trade agreements, as of 15 Oct 2026
    • Mauritius: CECPA in force since 2021
    • Most other markets: no India agreement. We check each one we shortlist.

    Southeast Asia

    Price-sensitive, fast-growing, strongly relationship-led.

    Demand drivers
    • Fast economic growth
    • Expanding manufacturing and infrastructure
    Buying behaviour
    • Price-sensitive
    • Strongly relationship-led
    • Trust is built over time, often in person
    Channel structure
    • Distributor and agent networks
    • Local relationships often decide access
    Entry considerations
    • Invest in relationships, not just price lists
    • Position on value, so you are not compared on price alone
    • Choose partners with proven local standing
    Typical risks
    • Price competition that erodes margin
    • Relationships that depend on one person
    • Slow first orders while trust builds
    Trade agreements, as of 15 Oct 2026
    • ASEAN: India-ASEAN free trade agreement in force

    Europe

    High-value but compliance- and standards-driven.

    Demand drivers
    • High-value industrial demand
    • A strong emphasis on quality and reliability
    Buying behaviour
    • Standards, documentation and certification shape decisions
    • Evaluations can be long and thorough
    Channel structure
    • Specialist distributors
    • Direct supply to manufacturers
    Entry considerations
    • Settle certification and standards requirements first
    • Prepare documentation to the buyer's standard
    • Price for a premium position, not a discount one
    Typical risks
    • Compliance gaps that block entry
    • Long approval cycles
    • Being treated as a low-price alternative
    Trade agreements, as of 15 Oct 2026
    • UK: CETA in force since 15 July 2026
    • EFTA: TEPA in force since 1 October 2025
    • EU: agreement concluded 27 January 2026, not yet in force

    North America

    Large and premium, with specification and certification gatekeepers.

    Demand drivers
    • A large, premium industrial market
    • Demand for qualified, specified products
    Buying behaviour
    • Specification-led
    • Approved-supplier lists and certification act as gates
    Channel structure
    • Distributors, representatives and specifiers act as gatekeepers
    Entry considerations
    • Plan the certification and specification path before volume
    • Win a specifier or distributor who can open doors
    • Budget for a longer qualification period
    Typical risks
    • Gatekeepers who decide who is even considered
    • Long qualification before the first order
    • High cost of entry if the path is not planned
    Trade agreements, as of 15 Oct 2026
    • US: bilateral trade agreement talks ongoing
    • Canada: CEPA negotiations ongoing

    Emerging Industrial Markets

    Early-mover opportunities where competition is thinner.

    Demand drivers
    • Industrial demand that is still building
    • Fewer established suppliers
    Buying behaviour
    • Varies widely
    • Fewer entrenched supplier relationships, so early movers can shape them
    Channel structure
    • Often undeveloped, or hard to see from outside
    Entry considerations
    • Test with a focused pilot, backed by evidence
    • Choose markets product by product, not by region
    • Keep commitments small until demand is proven
    Typical risks
    • Limited data on demand and pricing
    • Uncertain payment and regulation
    • Committing before the evidence exists
    Trade agreements, as of 15 Oct 2026
    • Varies by country. We check the position for each market we shortlist.
